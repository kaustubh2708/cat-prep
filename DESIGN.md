# DESIGN.md — Visual World & Craft Tokens (Impeccable Specification)

## Visual World
**Scholarly Monograph & Architectural Study Desk** (`Operate` + `Read` Modes)
- **Frame**: Deep Editorial Forest masthead (`#132A1E`) with warm parchment ink (`#F4F1EA`) and hue-tinted sage secondary text (`#A9BFB1`).
- **Ground & Paper**: Warm Parchment canvas (`#F5F2EB`) and crisp archival sheet surfaces (`#FCFBF8`) divided by `1px solid` stone hairlines (`#DFDBD2`) — never nested cards-inside-cards or ghost-card shadows.
- **Typography**:
  - **Display & Headings**: `Newsreader` (optical serif, weights 500–600, tracking `-0.02em`, `text-wrap: balance`).
  - **Reading & Controls**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (16px/17px base, line-height `1.65`, reading measure capped at `65ch–72ch`).
  - **Numerals, Timers & Codes**: `JetBrains Mono` (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Signature Move**:
  - **The 24-Skill Mastery Ledger & Split Monograph Sheet**: A two-pane interactive skill index where selecting any of the 24 CAT skills opens its structured 1-page formula & trap sheet alongside an immediate Easy/Medium practice queue and a 1-click confidence selector (`Needs Practice` · `Getting There` · `Confident`).

## Craft Floor Enforcement
- **No Emojis / Glyph Icons**: All iconography uses consistent 1.5px stroke inline SVGs.
- **No Eyebrows / Kickers**: Headings stand on their own typographic hierarchy without uppercase badges above them.
- **No Side-Stripe Borders**: Callouts use full 1px borders and subtle hue-matched surface tints.
- **No Ghost Cards**: Surfaces use either a 1px structural hairline border or an elevated floating shadow (for modals only), never both.
- **Browser Surfaces Themed**: Custom `::selection`, `caret-color`, `:focus-visible`, `text-underline-offset`, and scrollbars.
