# PRODUCT.md — CAT Final 6-Day Study Desk & Skill Ledger (2021–2025)

## Audience & Use Scene
- **User**: A CAT aspirant in the final 6 days before the exam working from a laptop in long, focused 45–60 minute sessions.
- **Emotional Goal**: Quiet confidence, clarity, and zero visual overwhelm. The interface must feel like an authoritative, calm editorial workbook and architectural study desk — never a noisy gamified SaaS dashboard.
- **Core Jobs**:
  1. **Follow a Realistic 6-Day Plan**: Work through 15–18 handpicked questions per day across QA, DILR, and VARC without staring at an intimidating 1,002-question backlog.
  2. **Master the 24 Core CAT Skills**: Browse all 24 exam skills (11 QA, 6 DILR, 7 VARC), rate personal confidence (`Needs Practice`, `Getting There`, `Confident`), review a 30-second formula & trap sheet for each skill, and launch a 3-question warmup on that exact skill.
  3. **Solve with Original Exam Fidelity**: Read the cropped original PDF diagram, chart, or mathematical equation side-by-side with clean interactive MCQ options (with option strike-out) or TITA input, a gentle timer, a 1-line starting hint, and verified step-by-step solutions.
  4. **Practice 40-Minute Section Selection**: Open any of the 15 actual CAT slots (2021–2025, Slots 1–3) to scan and pick Round-1 questions before starting a 40-minute clock.
